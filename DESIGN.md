# Interface direction

This is an Operate surface: a small, bright laboratory notebook with a fixed reading order from record setup to plate review. I chose it under the brief's delegation of design decisions.

I considered notebook ledgers, instrument control panels, reagent labels, scientific figure sheets, bench worksheets, specimen accession desks, and field sampling logs. The direction seed selected bench worksheets (candidate five): white paper, graphite rules, restrained green actions, human-readable well addresses, and a plate map beside the review. Airport signs lose assay context but contribute clear next actions; catalog identities lose field clarity but contribute disciplined alignment; measurement rails contribute honest scales to the curve chart.

Use a light background for the assumed bright bench environment. Text and controls use a humanist sans face, measurements use tabular numerals, headings stay sentence case. Sections are ruled rather than floating cards. Failures use words and burgundy, success uses words and deep green. On small screens, columns stack and the 96-well map scrolls inside its own labelled region. Every input has a visible label; status changes are announced. The chart shows standards and the fit rather than decorative summary numbers.
